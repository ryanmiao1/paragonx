## Description

This course is designed for students who are comfortable with Problems 1–15 of the AMC 8 and want to develop the skills needed to solve Problems 16–25. Students will learn frequently used techniques in algebra, geometry, number theory, combinatorics, and probability, with an emphasis on recognizing problem structures, selecting efficient strategies, and developing intuition for challenging AMC 8 problems. Each class will include guided problem solving and targeted practice, with homework assigned to reinforce the concepts covered.

## Syllabus

1. Algebraic Modeling
2. Advanced Word Problems
3. Number Theory
4. Advanced Geometry
5. Counting: Casework, Arrangements, and Path Counting
6. Probability and Logic
7. Mixed Challenge Problems
8. Full-Length Mock AMC 8 and Solution Workshop
