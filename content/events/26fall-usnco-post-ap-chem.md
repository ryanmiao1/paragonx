## Description

This course seeks to systematically cover the major topics on the USNCO Exams, both Local and Nationals, particularly focusing on extending into more difficult concepts and complete explanations beyond typical high school curricula.

## Syllabus

1. Descriptive Chemistry
2. Thermochemistry, Thermodynamics
3. States of Matter
4. Chemical Bonding Part 1
5. Chemical Bonding Part 2
6. Equilibrium, Complexation, and Problem-Solving Tricks
7. Kinetics and Electrochemistry
8. Basic Organic Chemistry
