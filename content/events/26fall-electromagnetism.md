## Description

This course is for students who are at around the AP Physics C level for Electromagnetism and want to master electromagnetism for physics olympiads such as USAPhO. Prerequisites are calculus and knowledge of AP Physics C: Electricity and Magnetism.

## Syllabus

1. Electrostatics
2. Electric Potential
3. Conductors
4. Magnetism
5. Induction
6. Maxwell Equations
7. Electric Fields in Matter
8. Magnetic Fields in Matter
