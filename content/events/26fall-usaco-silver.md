## Description

This eight-week course will cover the important topics to know for USACO Silver and is designed for students who are experienced in programming and USACO Bronze topics. Each class will follow the format of lecture mixed with solving and implementing problems. Homework will be assigned after each class, and we will review it at the beginning of the next class.

## Syllabus

1. Searching, Sorting, Greedy Algorithms
2. Data Structures
3. Graphs
4. Searching II
5. Greedy Algorithms II
6. Data Structures II
7. Graphs II
8. Graphs III
