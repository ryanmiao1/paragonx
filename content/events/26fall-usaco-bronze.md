## Description

This eight-week course will cover the important topics to know for USACO Bronze and is designed for students with basic knowledge of programming. Each class will follow the format of lecture followed by algorithmically solving and implementing problems. Homework will be assigned after each class, and we will review it at the beginning of the next class.

## Syllabus

1. Simulation 1
2. Simulation 2
3. Complete Search
4. Data Structures
5. Ad Hoc 1
6. Ad Hoc 2
7. Greedy Algorithms
8. Other Topics (Math, Casework, Geometry, Graphs, etc.)
